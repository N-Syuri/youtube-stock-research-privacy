# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 29, 2026**

## 1. Purpose of Use

This project is an academic research project investigating whether sentiment expressed in YouTube comments is associated with subsequent movements in Japanese stock prices.

YouTube Data API Services are used to collect publicly available comment data for research and analysis. The collected data may be processed using natural language processing and machine learning methods, including BERT-based sentiment analysis.

## 2. Information Collected

Depending on the API requests made, this project may collect publicly available YouTube comment text and associated metadata, such as video identifiers, comment identifiers, publication timestamps, and reply information.

The project does not intentionally collect passwords, private messages, or other non-public account information.

## 3. Use of Information

The collected information is used for academic research, including text analysis, sentiment scoring, and statistical analysis of Japanese stock market movements.

Research outputs may include aggregated statistics and independently generated sentiment metrics. These metrics are calculated by the researcher and are not official YouTube metrics or endorsements by YouTube.

## 4. Storage and Retention

YouTube API data will be stored, refreshed, and deleted in accordance with the applicable YouTube API Services Terms of Service and Developer Policies, including applicable retention limits.

Any extended retention of statistical data or derived metrics will be subject to the applicable YouTube policies and any required approvals.

## 5. Sharing and Publication

Research findings may be presented in a master's thesis or academic presentation. Individual comment text and identifying information will not be published unless permitted by applicable policies and law.
